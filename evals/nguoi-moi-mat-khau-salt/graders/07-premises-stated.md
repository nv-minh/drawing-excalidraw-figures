You understand the subject, so do not judge whether you could follow the figure: a model that understands every word cannot tell what a real reader lacks.
Judge what the figure takes for granted.
Walk the image in reading order.
At each step write what the reader must already accept for the step to make sense: a term, a rule of how a part normally works, a relation between two things the image names.
The reader is a parent who has never programmed and knows everyday words only.
PASS if every such item is said on the image in plain words, or the image still reads without it.
FAIL if a step cannot be followed without an item the image never says (a number that cannot be checked, a step whose reason is missing), even when every term on the image has a gloss.
